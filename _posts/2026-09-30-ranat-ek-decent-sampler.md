I'm fortunate to have friends who think of me while traveling, and who keep bringing me musical instruments from their journeys. How lucky is that?

This is Ranat Ek, a decorative toy xylophone I was given many years ago. Having sampled it and put it into a preset for the excellent [Decent Sampler](https://www.decentsamples.com/product/decent-sampler-plugin/) you too can enjoy toying with it.
